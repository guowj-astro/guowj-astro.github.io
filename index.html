About

I study how accreting black holes change over time.

My research focuses on active galactic nuclei and intermediate-mass black-hole candidates. I use changes in their spectra and brightness to explore the physics of accretion and the gas around them.

I work with multi-epoch optical spectroscopy from SDSS and DESI, together with X-ray, radio, and photometric observations, to connect what changes across time and wavelength.

01

CHANGING-LOOK AGN

Watching active galaxies change.

I use repeat optical spectroscopy to study changes in broad Balmer emission lines and the evolving state of active galactic nuclei.

Multi-epoch spectroscopy · SDSS & DESI

02

BLACK HOLE DEMOGRAPHICS

Searching the intermediate-mass regime.

I investigate intermediate-mass black-hole candidates by bringing spectroscopic evidence together with X-ray, radio, and photometric information.

Black-hole candidates · Multiwavelength observations

03

EMISSION-LINE VARIABILITY

Following the narrow-line response.

I explore variability in narrow [O III] emission to investigate how the line-emitting gas responds to changes in an active nucleus.

[O III] variability · Time-domain astronomy
