# Wei-Jian Guo · Academic website

Public website: https://guowj-astro.github.io/

The compiled site is served by GitHub Pages from the root of the master branch.

The NGC 1566 host now combines a thin stellar disk with a broader, soft thick-disk component, a warm central bulge and a darker equatorial dust lane. Its side-view shape is inspired by [ESO’s observation of the edge-on galaxy NGC 4565](https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0525a/). The host midplane remains aligned with the dusty torus and broad-line region, and the photograph’s nucleus remains at the scene origin. Added depth is illustrative, not a measured three-dimensional reconstruction of either galaxy.

The AGN nucleus, dusty clouds, narrow-line region, camera, navigation, research content and quantitative response models are retained. Reduced-motion preferences and offscreen rendering suspension remain supported.

Photo credit: ESA/Hubble & NASA. Acknowledgement: Det58. [NGC 1566 source](https://esahubble.org/images/potw1422a/) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The original NGC 1566 JPEG is unchanged; its colours and spiral texture are displayed with illustrative depth, local brightness adjustments and independent AGN overlays. Nuclear structures are not resolved features of the photograph. The NGC 4565 image is a visual reference and is not bundled.
