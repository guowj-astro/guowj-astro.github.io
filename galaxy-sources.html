OBSERVED GALAXIES / IMAGE COLLECTION

A deeper, more varied sky.

32 distinct galaxy photographs from the ESA/Hubble archive: spiral, barred, edge-on, elliptical, lenticular, ring, polar-ring, irregular and interacting systems. Some observations show a close-up region or multiple interacting galaxies.

The interactive background uses illustrative sizes, positions and depth layers, rather than measured distances. Photos are rotated, edge-feathered, scaled and brightness-weighted in the scene. The source images shown below remain unmodified.
